Your customers already use WhatsApp every day, so it is natural to want to send them offers, reminders and updates there. But businesses that send messages carelessly often lose their number. Here is how to use WhatsApp for marketing in a way that works and keeps your account safe.

## Three ways to use WhatsApp for business

**The normal WhatsApp app.** Fine for chatting with a few customers. It is not built for sending to many people, and sending bulk messages from it is the fastest way to get blocked.

**The WhatsApp Business app.** A free app for small shops, with a business profile, quick replies and labels. Better, but still limited for large campaigns.

**The WhatsApp Business Platform (official API).** Made for sending messages at scale, connecting to your customer system and automating updates such as order confirmations. This is the official route for bulk messaging, and it comes with rules.

## The rules that keep your number safe

These are the ideas behind WhatsApp's rules. Policies change, so always check WhatsApp's current terms.

1. **Get permission first.** Message people who agreed to hear from you, for example when they filled a form, placed an order or asked you to contact them. Do not send to numbers you bought or collected from somewhere else.
2. **Use approved templates for first contact.** On the official platform, messages you start are usually sent using templates that WhatsApp reviews in advance.
3. **Reply freely when they write to you.** When a customer messages you, you can generally reply normally for a limited window after their last message. After that window closes, you use a template again.
4. **Make it easy to stop.** Include a simple way to opt out and respect it straight away.
5. **Send things people want.** If many people block or report your number, WhatsApp lowers your quality rating and can limit or ban you.

## What works well

- Order and delivery updates
- Appointment reminders
- Replies to enquiries
- Occasional offers to people who asked to hear about them
- Short, clear messages that get to the point

## Common mistakes

- Sending the same message to everyone on a long list
- Messaging people who never asked
- No way to opt out
- Sending too often
- Using a personal number for campaigns

## Start small

Begin with one useful message to customers who have already agreed, such as an order update. See how people respond, then add more. Connecting WhatsApp to your customer records means messages can be sent automatically and replies do not get lost.
