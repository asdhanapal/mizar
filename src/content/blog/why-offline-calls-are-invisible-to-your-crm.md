Many sales teams notice the same pattern: answer rates on VoIP dialers drop, so agents quietly switch to their personal mobile phones. The customer picks up and the deal moves forward — but the call never appears in the CRM.

## Why VoIP calls fail

Telecom networks increasingly flag unfamiliar VoIP numbers as likely spam. Customers reject the call, or it never rings. Agents respond rationally: they use the phone that works.

## The hidden cost

When calls happen outside the system, managers lose:

- **Recordings** to coach agents and review disputes
- **Call logs** with duration and outcome
- **Accountability** for follow-ups

## What good looks like

The fix is not to force agents back onto a dialer that does not work. It is to capture the calls where they already happen. An Android app such as [CORE](/core/) detects calls, records them, uploads the audio to cloud storage and syncs the metadata to the CRM automatically.

Recording laws differ by country and state, so make sure your process obtains the consent required where you operate.
