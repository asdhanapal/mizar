## Apps that do real work

We build Android apps for businesses that need more than a brochure on a phone. Our own product, CORE, detects calls in the background, records them, uploads the audio to cloud storage and syncs the details to a CRM. That kind of background, always-on behaviour is what we specialise in.

## What we can build

- Team tools for sales, field service and support
- Apps that detect events on the phone and act on them automatically
- Offline-first apps that sync when the phone is online
- Apps connected to your CRM, cloud storage and back-end

## How we work

1. **Discovery.** Who will use the app, on which phones, and what it must do on day one.
2. **Build in steps.** Working versions you can install and try every couple of weeks.
3. **Test on real devices.** Different phones and Android versions behave differently.
4. **Launch and improve.** Release, monitor and keep improving.
