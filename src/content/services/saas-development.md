## From idea to paying customers

A SaaS product is more than screens. It is accounts, permissions, billing, reporting and an infrastructure that stays up. We build all of it as one coherent system so you are not stitching parts together later.

## How we work

1. **Discovery.** Users, goals, constraints and a written scope.
2. **Build.** Short iterations with working software you can use every week.
3. **Launch.** Deployment, monitoring and hand-over documentation.
4. **Grow.** Continuous improvement, paired with our marketing team when you need customers as well as code.
