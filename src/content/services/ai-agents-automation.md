## What an agent actually does

Most "AI chatbot" products answer one question and stop. An agent is different: it reads something that arrives (a WhatsApp message, a call recording, a form submission), works out what should happen next, and does it, across the systems you already use, not just inside a chat window.

Because we already build the CRM, WhatsApp and call-recording integrations most AI agent projects get stuck wiring up, our agents can take real action from day one rather than just producing a suggestion for someone to copy and paste.

## Where this fits what we already build

- A **lead-qualifying agent** that reads a WhatsApp or web enquiry, asks the missing details, and logs a scored lead straight into your CRM.
- A **call-outcome agent** that reads a CORE call transcript and tags the result (interested, not interested, call back) on the right CRM record.
- A **support-triage agent** that reads an incoming message, drafts a reply, and routes anything it is not confident about to a person.
- An **ops agent** for a SaaS product: chasing overdue invoices, sending renewal reminders, or flagging churn risk before you lose the account.

## How we work

1. **Discovery.** Which process is repetitive, what "good" looks like, and which systems the agent needs to read from and write to.
2. **Scope and data rules.** A written scope, a fixed estimate, and clear agreement on what data the agent can see and where it hands off to a person.
3. **Build and test.** The agent runs alongside your current process first, so you can check its decisions before it acts on its own.
4. **Launch and monitor.** A log of every action it takes, so you can see what it did and adjust the rules as you learn.
