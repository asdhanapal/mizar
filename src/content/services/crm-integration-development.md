## Your CRM is only as good as the information in it

Most CRMs are half empty. Calls happen on personal phones, leads arrive on WhatsApp, forms sit in an inbox, and someone has to type it all in. Skandava connects those sources to your CRM automatically so the record is complete without extra work from your team.

## What we build

- Website forms and WhatsApp messages that create or update leads
- Call logging and recordings attached to the right customer (our CORE app does this for Android phones)
- Two-way sync, so changes in one place appear in the other
- Custom reports and dashboards for managers

## How we work

1. **Discovery.** How your team sells today and which CRM you use.
2. **Plan.** A short written scope and a fixed estimate.
3. **Build and test.** Working integrations you can try on real data.
4. **Hand over.** Documentation and support while your team settles in.
