## Engineering that fits the business problem

We do not start with a framework, we start with the problem. Our engineers scope the smallest system that solves it, build it with maintainable architecture, and leave you with software your team can own.

## How we work

1. **Discovery** — goals, users, constraints and a written scope.
2. **Build** — short iterations with working software you can click through every week.
3. **Launch** — deployment, monitoring and hand-over documentation.
4. **Grow** — continuous improvement, paired with our marketing team when you need traffic as well as code.
