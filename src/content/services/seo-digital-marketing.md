## SEO is part engineering, part editorial

Search rankings depend on three things: content that matches what people want, technical foundations that let Google crawl and understand it, and authority earned over time. We handle all three.

## Our approach

1. **Audit** — crawl, index coverage, Core Web Vitals and content gaps.
2. **Fix the foundations** — rendering, speed, structured data, internal linking.
3. **Publish with intent** — pages and articles mapped to real search demand.
4. **Measure** — Search Console and analytics reviewed against business goals, not vanity metrics.
