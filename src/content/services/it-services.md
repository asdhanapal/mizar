## Technology that helps you grow, not just keeps the lights on

Skandava is an engineering-led team. We build software, websites and mobile apps, run cloud systems on AWS, connect your customer conversations to your CRM, and help people find you on Google. Working as one team means your website, your systems and your marketing fit together.

## How we work

1. **Listen.** What you want to achieve, in your own words.
2. **Recommend.** The simplest thing that does the job, with a clear scope and estimate.
3. **Build.** Working software in short steps you can see and try.
4. **Support.** Launch, monitoring and improvement.
